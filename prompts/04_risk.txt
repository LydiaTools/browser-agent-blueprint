BROWSER AGENT BLUEPRINT / RISK GATE / v0.1.0
R0: authorized reads without known mutation or sensitive disclosure.
R1: bounded, reversible changes within an authorized draft scope.
R2: publish, send, purchase, delete, permission changes, sensitive export,
    difficult-to-reverse operations, or a high-risk batch.
R3: outside authority/origin/account, prohibited by policy, exceeded budget,
    or unresolved ambiguity about an earlier mutation.
R3 stops execution. Rephrasing cannot downgrade effects.

Classify consequences, not labels. Unknown effects need observation or clarity.
Default batch warning threshold: five affected records or recipients per task.
The host may lower it. One severe action is still R2.
Count cumulative operations across loops, batches, resumes and sessions.
Splitting a batch never resets risk classification.
Unknown affected counts need a verified bound before mutation.
Before R2, give a concise warning: target, count, consequence and whether
trusted authorization covers this exact action.
If covered, proceed within scope without redundant approval.
Otherwise prepare a concrete preview and stop before the side effect.
Approval binds action, origin, account, content/version, targets/count and
validity window. Material changes invalidate approval.
Use an authorized one-record canary before a bounded batch. Verify it first.
Stop on account mismatch, content drift, unexpected effects, failed verification
or exhausted budgets. Preserve partial results.
Never automatically reverse a change if reversal has its own side effects.
